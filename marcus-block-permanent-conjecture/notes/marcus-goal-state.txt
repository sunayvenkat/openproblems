CERTIFICATE INTERPRETATION (fully specified in the paper):
For a cut c, take Q_1...Q_c holomorphic, other Q's antiholomorphic.
One raw covariant has B=mk-2 free indices, L=kc-1 holomorphic and the rest
antiholomorphic. Its networks field gives k legs of each factor and delta
pairs, with t>=1 internal hol/anti contractions and t-1 delta lifts.
Block integer matrix C gives tensor combinations V_a=sum_n C_na T_n.
Block rational PD matrix H gives S_b=sum_ab H_ab <V_a,V_b>.
Exact identity F=(1/m!) sum_{permutations of block labels} sum_b S_b.
F is Fock product norm squared minus permanent of polynomial Gram matrix.

Standalone verifier uses only Node built-ins and BigInt rational arithmetic.
It enumerates 8! or 9! Wick pairings, checks all orbit coefficients, proves
all H positive definite by exact LDL, and checks equality completeness by

Counts: 4,2,6 ->282 Wick tables,25 orbits,15 PD blocks,spanning rank360.
        3,3,9 ->55 Wick tables,16 orbits,11 PD blocks,spanning rank320.
Equality: PD makes all V=0; complete boundary-permutation span gives every
raw T=0. A t=1 raw tensor is a pairwise one-index contraction times nonzero
other tensors, hence all essential-variable support spaces are orthogonal.

APPLICATION: Cubic case directly uses rank <=9 for nine Gram vectors.
Four-pair case projects last pair onto U=span(first six vectors), dim<=6.
F=F(first3,p0)+sum_alpha(||f h_alpha||^2-q||h_alpha||^2), q=perGram(first3).

CORE DISCOVERY:
For three arbitrary complex symmetric quadratic matrices Q1,Q2,Q3 on C^5,
p_i=z^T Q_i z and Gij=2 tr(Q_i^* Q_j), put F=||p1 p2 p3||_Fock^2-per G.
For each distinguished C, others A,B:
T_abcd=A_ab(BC*)_cd+B_ab(AC*)_cd;
S_abcd=T_abcd+T_acbd+T_bcad; H=3T-S;
W=AC*B+BC*A; L=A tr(BC*)+B tr(AC*).
E(X)_abcd=delta_ad X_bc+delta_bd X_ac+delta_cd X_ab;
K(X)_abcd=delta_ad X_bc+delta_bd X_ac-2 delta_cd X_ab.
Exact positive identity in dimension five:
189 F=sum_C [16||7S-E(L+2W)||^2+7||4H+K(L-W)||^2
             +378||W||^2+126||2L+5W||^2].

The raw identity, valid in all dimensions, is
27F=sum_C[112||S||^2+16||H||^2+288||W||^2+216 Re<W,L>].
marcus-quadratic-identity.mjs verifies it by exact symbolic Einstein
contraction counting, independently of numerical fits: all 15 nontrivial
3x3 incidence tables, representing 672 permutations; 48 wreath permutations
are exactly per G. Counts and a five-row grouped table are in its JSON.

The delta trace identities in the proof convert the raw identity into the
positive identity. Explicit integer-scaled tensor norms were additionally
checked on 40 complex integer matrix samples of dimension five by
marcus-quadratic-sos.mjs. These sample fits are NOT the proof.
The proof uses exact contraction counting plus elementary delta identities.

RANK REDUCTION (proved in paper, not assumed from web):
For six original Gram vectors, let U be the span of first four (dim<=4).
Expand the third block polynomial in orthogonal outside Gaussian coordinates:
p3=p0+sum_{alpha!=0}h_alpha w^alpha/sqrt(alpha!).
F=F(p1,p2,p0)+sum_alpha(||p1p2 h_alpha||^2-per Gram(p1,p2)||h_alpha||^2).
Every extra summand is nonnegative by normal-ordering/Bombieri and the
two-polynomial permanent bound. The first bracket uses the C^5 SOS.
Equality follows from the SOS kernel and the strict linear multiplier identity.
Detailed equality proof and normal-ordering derivation are in the paper.
