# Four responses sharpen Erdős’s remaining return

**Four fixed actual response constants now describe the leading signed approximation error, and a two-node Farey argument broadens nonvanishing in five compatible state classes.** The second result accommodates unbounded successor ratios without a restriction on the following continued-fraction digit. Both advances survive fresh independent audits. Their arithmetic interface remains unfinished: the four constants are real weighted ray profiles whose rationality has not been shown to follow from rationality of the target. A separate theorem prices one proposed toric cancellation family above the currently proved supply budget. **No adequate infinite arithmetic return has been constructed; Erdős #168(ii) remains open in this work.** ([Response audit](<response_audit.md>), [state audit](<states_audit.md>), [cost audit](<cheaper_audit.md>))

Let \(c\) denote the limiting optimal density of sets avoiding \(\{n,2n,3n\}\), and let \(\eta_{a,b}\in\{0,1\}\) be the established finite-optimum birth digits. The completed combinatorial reduction gives
\[
Z=\sum_{a,b\ge0}c_{a,b}2^{-a}3^{-b}=9c-6,
\qquad c_{a,b}=1-3\eta_{a,b}.
\]
Proving \(Z\) irrational therefore proves the density irrational. Complete one-sided rational models \(\widetilde Z^\pm_{p,q}\), including their origin, low-row and isolate/patch corrections, have a known integer clearing factor
\[
D_{p,q}=27(2^{3q}-1)(3^{3p}-1)
\]
when \(p+q\not\equiv0\pmod3\). At convergents, its binary exponent is \(6q+o(q)\). The remaining challenge is to construct nonzero cleared errors tending to zero. ([Accepted foundation and scalar arithmetic](<../signed-arithmetic-returns/REPORT.md>))

The quantitative comparison keeps both infinite exteriors. For a reduced rational model in state \(R\) with \(3/5<p/q<2/3\), suppose \(A\le q\), \(B\le p\), and its direct floors through \(A-1\) and reciprocal floors through \(B-1\) agree with the actual slope. The complete toric quotient satisfies

For consecutive actual convergents \(p/q,P/Q\), put \(r=p+P\), \(s=q+Q\), \(\sigma=\operatorname{sign}(\alpha-p/q)\), and \(\Delta=2^{-s}-3^{-r}\). A balanced nonresonant mediant shares the necessary floors through \(s-1,r-1\), so the comparison applies with \(A=s,B=r\). The complete error bound is
\[
\left|(Z-\widetilde Z^\sigma_{p,q})-\sigma\Delta G_R\right|
\le R_1+|\Delta|\left(\frac{45}{2}2^{-s}+\frac{63}{2}3^{-r}\right),
\]
where \(R_1=\mathcal V(p,q;r,s)+\mathcal V(r,s;P,Q)\). Here the entire open-Farey variation is bounded, for \(|ad-bc|=1\), by
\[
\mathcal V(a,b;c,d)=9\left[
2^{1-A}\left(1+\frac{A+1}{bd}\right)
+3^{-B}\left(\frac32+\frac{\frac32B+\frac34}{ac}\right)\right],
\quad A=b+d,\ B=a+c.
\]
Consequently the error is
\[
Z-\widetilde Z^\sigma_{p,q}
=\sigma(2^{-s}-3^{-r})G_R+O(2^{-(s+q)}).
\]
This is an **absolute-error expansion**. An exceptionally small physical gap can leave the leading term below its remainder; nonzero \(G_R\) alone does not establish nonvanishing. ([Complete charged expansion, RR7–RR8](<response_relations.md>))
