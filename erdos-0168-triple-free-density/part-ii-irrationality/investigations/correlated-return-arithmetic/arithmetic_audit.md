### Inferences

**Primary gcd specialization.** Take (u=2^{3q},v=3^{3p}). They lie in the fixed group generated by ((2,1)) and ((1,3)); a relation (u^rv^s=1) forces (r=s=0) by the valuations at 2 and 3. Thus the dependence exceptions do not apply. Since (plog3=qlog2+o(1)), the primary bound gives

\[
\log\gcd(A,B)=o(q).
\tag{A1}
\]

The full gcd is coprime to six: (A) is odd and (B\equiv-1\pmod3). Hence there is no hidden discrepancy between the off-(\{2,3\}) gcd and the full integer gcd in this application. The bound supplies no effective finite numerical threshold. — [Primary Corollary 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0311030v2#page=2), [earlier accepted specialization](../response-closure-tests/cheaper_audit.md).

**Third coefficient.** Its numerator is (VB-UA), with denominator (UVAB). Eventually (Q>v_2(B)=O(\log p)) and (P>v_3(A)=O(\log q)). Unequal valuations then give

\[
v_2(VB-UA)=v_2(B),\qquad v_3(VB-UA)=v_3(A).
\]

The exact smooth denominator is consequently (2^Q3^P). If (A_0=A/3^{v_3(A)}, B_0=B/2^{v_2(B)}), and (c=\gcd(A_0,B_0)), the actual reduced denominator satisfies

\[
UV\,\frac{A_0B_0}{c^2}\mid\operatorname{den}(C_3)
\mid UV A_0B_0.
\tag{A2}
\]

The first divisibility is valid even at equal prime valuations: its proposed exponent is zero there. At unequal valuations the reduced denominator retains the absolute difference of the two axis valuations. Combining A1–A2 proves (\log_2\operatorname{den}(C_3)=2Q+6q+o(q)); it also proves the same exponent for the least common denominator of the three coefficients. — [SS3–SS4](same_scale.md), [independent smooth-part and divisor checks](arithmetic_audit_check.py).

**General fixed polynomial.** Clear the fixed rational content once. Let

\[
\ell=\operatorname{ord}_0P_g,\qquad
F=\gcd(P_g,t^3-1),\qquad d=\deg F,\qquad
H=(t^3-1)/F.
\]

Writing (P_g=t^\ell FR), the fixed polynomials (R,H) are coprime. A fixed polynomial Bézout identity bounds each evaluated gcd (\gcd(R(a),H(a))) and (\gcd(R(b),H(b))), up to fixed content factors. Since (H(0)=\pm1) and (R(0)\ne0), the first summand's large smooth denominator is (2^{[Q-\ell q]_+}) up to factors with logarithm (O(\log q)); the second has (3^{[P-\ell p]_+}) up to the same size of error. All opposite-axis smooth valuations divide (A) or (B), so LTE bounds their logarithms by (O(\log q)). The nonsmooth denominator of each summand has binary logarithm ((3-d)q+o(q)). Fixed rational coefficient denominators contribute only fixed factors. — [SS5 proof](same_scale.md), [exact finite monomial and cyclotomic cases](arithmetic_audit_check.py).

The two reduced summand denominators have gcd with logarithm (o(q)): the smooth contribution is at most the small opposite-axis valuations; the remaining contribution is bounded by A1. For reduced summand denominators (d_1,d_2), the reduced denominator of their difference is divisible by (d_1d_2/\gcd(d_1,d_2)^2) and divides (d_1d_2). This follows prime by prime and allows arbitrarily strong cancellation when the two valuations coincide. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\log_2\operatorname{den}\!\left(\sum C_jg_j\right)
=2[Q-\ell q]_++2(3-d)q+o(q).}
\tag{A3}
\]

The positive-part conversion is valid because

\[
(P-\ell p)\log3-(Q-\ell q)\log2=o(1).
\]

It remains valid at the boundary (Q\approx\ell q); no unjustified assertion that all successor prime powers survive is needed. Since (\ell+d\le2), A3 gives (\log_2\operatorname{den}(\sum C_jg_j)\ge2Q+2q-o(q)). If (Q<\ell q), then (Q>q) forces (\ell=2,d=0), and the formula gives (6q\ge2Q+2q). This explicitly checks the potentially delicate (Q<2q) regime. — [SS5–SS6](same_scale.md), [independent lower-divisor checks](arithmetic_audit_check.py).

For an actual fan triple, the independently enclosed nonzero components, mixed signs, and nonzero sum are inputs from the parallel analytic audit. **If that triple is rational**, its polynomial has (\ell=0); its nonzero sum excludes (t-1), and mixed signs exclude a multiple of (t^2+t+1). Thus (d=0) and its correction alone has exponent (2Q+6q+o(q)). This does not rationalize the actual response values and does not follow merely from rational (Z). — [Complete fan enclosures](farey_fan.md), [SS5's explicit conditional application](same_scale.md).

### Inferences

Under the additional hypothesis (G_j=h_j/T), with fixed (h_j\in\mathbb Z) and (T\in\mathbb Z_{>0}), common-denominator arithmetic gives exactly

\[
z_*=z+\frac{\sigma}{T}\left(\frac{P_h(a)}{UA}-\frac{P_h(b)}{VB}\right)
=\frac{J}{L},
\]
\[
L=27TUVAB,\qquad
J=TUVN+27\sigma\bigl(VB P_h(a)-UA P_h(b)\bigr),
\]
\[
b_*:=\operatorname{den}(z_*)=\frac{L}{\gcd(|J|,L)}.
\tag{A4}
\]

The known full model is thus retained, and (\gcd(|J|,L)) is a new whole-numerator question. A bound for the fan correction's denominator does not bound this gcd. The independent checker verifies A4 with rational trial vectors having several distinct fixed denominators; these vectors are algebra checks, not assertions about the actual (G_j). — [SS7–SS8](same_scale.md), [independent normalization tests](arithmetic_audit_check.py).

Let (D=\gcd(|J|,L)). Since (UVAB=2^{2Q+6q}(1+o(1))),

\[
M_*:=b_*2^{-(q+2Q)}
=27T(1+o(1))\frac{2^{5q}}D.
\tag{A5}
\]

The analytic theorem implies (b_*|Z-z_*|<300M_*). Consequently the currently supplied **upper-bound expression** tends to zero exactly when (D/2^{5q}\to\infty). A5 is not an asymptotic for the actual error, which can be smaller or zero. Its constant with the actual guaranteed fan bound is (8100T), rather than (27T); the source correctly calls the (27T) expression a unit normalized majorant. No correction of its mathematical claim is needed. — [SS9](same_scale.md), [FF5–FF6](farey_fan.md).

The corrected residual also needs nonvanishing on infinitely many applicable indices. Nonvanishing of (Z-z) does not transfer to (Z-z_*) after subtracting a fan correction. The arithmetic interface therefore still needs rational response values under the hypothetical rational target, sufficient full-numerator gcd saving or a genuinely stronger correlated error, an infinite applicable family, and corrected-residual nonvanishing. None is supplied by A3–A5. — [Explicit SS9 gaps](same_scale.md), [scope of scalar nonvanishing](../weighted-response-identities/REPORT.md).
