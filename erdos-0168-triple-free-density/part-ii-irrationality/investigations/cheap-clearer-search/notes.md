# Erdős 168(ii): search for cheap clearers, and why the tripling is intrinsic

Date: 2026-11-09. **Status: part (ii) is NOT proved here.** No new infinite theorem was
obtained. This note records two negative numerical probes, one derivation, one reformulation,
and the obstruction they leave. Labels: [D] derivation from the working inputs (not audited),
[C] finite computation (scripts in this folder), [H] heuristic reading.

Notation as in ERDOS168_PROGRESS.md: alpha = log2/log3, p/q and P/Q consecutive convergents,
W = q+Q the agreement window, D = 27(2^{3q}-1)(3^{3p}-1).

## 1. Why a digit-free proof needs a cost below the window [D]

Every slope model costs log2 D = 6q against an error exponent W = (a_{n+1}+1)q + q_{n-1}.
A digit-free argument must work at indices with a_{n+1} in {1,2}, where W < 4q. It therefore
needs a clearer of cost < W there, roughly 2q to 3q. The six-state nonvanishing theorems are
irrelevant to this: the shortfall is in the cost, not in nonvanishing.

L_* implies that alpha has infinitely many partial quotients >= 5. That is open and not
approachable with known methods. A proof of (ii) through the present family is therefore a
proof about the continued fraction of log2/log3. Sections 2 and 3 test whether a cheaper
clearer is hiding.

## 2. Probe 1: sub-cyclotomic denominators [C]

Script cheap.py. Z is taken from the stored 15601/24727 model (true precision about 75000 bits,
20000 used). For p/q in {5/8, 12/19, 41/65, 53/84, 306/485, 665/1054} it evaluates ||dZ|| for

    d = m * 2^x * (product of any subset of {2^q-1, 2^{2q}+2^q+1, 3^p-1, 3^{2p}+3^p+1})
    d = m * 2^x * e   or   m * 2^x * e * f

where e is one of 2^q 3^p - 1, |2^q-3^p|, |2^{3q}-3^{3p}|, 2^{2q}+2^q 3^p+3^{2p}, 2^q 3^p+1,
2^{2q}3^{2p}+2^q 3^p+1, f is one cyclotomic factor, m is in {1,3,9,27} and
0 <= x < min(3q,600). That is between 4416 and 110400 denominators per slope. A chance hit has
-log2||dZ|| about log2(#tests) <= 17. Threshold used: 22.

Result: the only hits are the known full clearer at 53/84 (71 bits, the documented +71 margin)
and its trivial 2^x and 27/9 variants. No proper sub-product of D, and none of the mixed
S-unit forms, clears Z at any tested slope.

## 3. Probe 2: lattice search over S-unit polynomials [C]

Script lll.py. Z from the stored 79335/125743 model (301900 bits used). For p/q in
{12/19, 41/65, 53/84, 306/485, 665/1054, 15601/24727, 31867/50508} LLL is run on the lattice
of integer vectors c = (c_ij), 0 <= i,j <= 3, with last coordinate
2^160 * ||sum c_ij 2^{iq} 3^{jp} Z||. A structured cheap clearer would appear as a vector with
O(1) coefficients and near-zero last coordinate. Generic size is 160/17, about 9 bits per
coordinate.

Result: every reduced basis is generic (coefficients 9-10 bits, last coordinate 1-7 bits) at
every slope, including the digit-2 scales 485, 24727, 50508 and the digit-1 scale 65. There is
no small-coefficient relation of degree <= 3 in (2^q, 3^p). The true clearer at 53/84 is not
the shortest vector at T=160 because its margin is only 71 bits; Probe 1 sees it.

Reading [H]: together with the Khinchin-like continued fraction of Z away from the 665/1054
spike, this says Z has no visible structured approximant cheaper than its window at small-digit
scales, within the monomial span generated by 2^q and 3^p. It does not exclude clearers built
from other data (earlier convergents, resonant W-1 factors, non-monomial constructions).

## 4. The tripling is three isotypic components, not an artefact [D]

For the raw colour sum S(u) = sum over 2^a 3^b <= 3^u of omega^{a-b} (corrections I, P
omitted), put N = floor(u/alpha) and kappa = 1/(1-omega^{-1}). Summing the inner geometric
series,

    S(u) = kappa [ sum_{a<=N} omega^a  -  omega^{-1} T(u) ],
    T(u) = sum_{a<=N} omega^{a - floor(u - a alpha)}.

In the slope-p/q model let X(u) = sum_{a<q} omega^{a - floor(u - ap/q)}. Then exactly

    T(u+p) = omega^{q} T(u) + omega^{-p} X(u),      X(u+p) = omega^{-p} X(u).

With sigma = p+q mod 3 nonzero, T = T_0 + X/(1-omega^sigma) where T_0(u+p) = omega^q T_0(u).
So under one model period u -> u+p the state is a sum of three pieces transforming by the
characters 1, omega^q, omega^{-p}:

    S = c_0(N mod 3) + E_1 + E_2.

When 3 does not divide pq these are the three distinct characters of Z/3. The minimising
colour is a nonlinear function of S, so it has genuine period 3q; no twisted q-periodicity of
the birth array exists. At sigma = 0 the two nontrivial characters coincide and the formula
degenerates into the known linear drift.

Consequence: a clearer of the form (2^q - omega^i)(3^p - omega^j) in Z[omega] cannot clear a
single Eisenstein-valued approximant of the real number Z; only the full norm does. Probe 1
agrees (no cyclotomic sub-product clears Z).

## 5. Half-cleared reformulation [D]

The birth digit is F(A_a + B_b + corr), with A depending on a only and B on b only. Replace
only the horizontal walk by its 3q-periodic model. The error is still at most C 2^{-(q+Q)},
because the first altered column is a = q+Q. With L = 3q this gives

    (2^L - 1) Z = W_n + O(2^{2q-Q}),
    W_n = sum_b 3^{-b} G_n(B_b) + (low-row terms),
    G_n(t) = sum_{a<L} 2^{L-a} F(A'_a + t + corr),   an integer with 0 <= G_n <= 2^{L+1}.

The error tends to zero whenever a_{n+1} >= 2 (using q_{n-1} -> infinity), not only for
a_{n+1} >= 5. If Z = A/B then ||B W_n|| -> 0 along all nonresonant indices with a_{n+1} >= 2.
So (ii) would follow from: for every B, ||B W_n|| is bounded below along infinitely many such n.

This is a trade, not a gain. W_n is a base-3 series with integer digits as large as 2^{3q+1},
so carries hide its arithmetic. Lemma C of theory.md shows that even nonresonant digits >= 2
are not forced by transcendence. The reformulation is recorded because it isolates what a
digit-2 proof would have to control: the ternary digit series of the vertical walk read through
the monotone integer kernel G_n.

## 6. Arguments tried and dropped

1. Eisenstein clearers of cost 2q: dead by Section 4 and Probe 1.
2. Hecke-Mahler and Mahler functional equations: the convergent monomial maps send (1/2,1/3) to
   (2^{-q}3^{p}, 2^{q'}3^{-p'}), which tends to (1,1), the singular corner, not to 0. The
   method degenerates exactly because the weights are constant along the ordering lines.
3. Adamczewski-Bugeaud stammering with free S-unit preperiods: the Subspace theorem discounts
   S-unit factors only when excluding algebraic irrationals. The rational case still pays the
   full archimedean cost, and a two-base rational total does not force periodicity (limit 3 of
   the progress file).
4. Removing the state-class restriction by "all late adequate models are equal": rational Z
   forces den(z_n) = den(Z) on every adequate family, i.e. total cancellation of
   D_n/gcd(D_n,B) from N_n. Disproving that needs N_n modulo a primitive prime, which has no
   visible structure (A3 of theory.md). No proof found.
5. Simultaneous approximation of the translated readouts and Nesterenko-type criteria: they
   bound the dimension of a span containing Z, never the irrationality of Z itself.
6. 2-adic and 3-adic limits of z_n: the vertical row sums are rationals with denominator
   3^{3p}-1 and need not converge 2-adically, so the S-adic meaning of z_n asserted in
   theory.md 6.1 looks weaker than stated. The identification obstruction recorded there stands
   in any case. This remark was not checked numerically.

## 6b. Second round (same date)

[C] isotypic_check.py verifies the two recurrences of Section 4 exactly in Eisenstein-integer
arithmetic: 6893 sample points over the slopes 5/8, 12/19, 41/65, 53/84, 7/11, 2/3, 19/30,
zero failures. The check covers the raw colour sum only.

[D] One-walk formula. Using S(2t) = omega S(t) + g(floor(log_3 2t)) and S = 1 on [1,2), the
gauge integral becomes

    integral_3^infty H(S(t)) t^{-2} dt = log3 * sum_a 2^{-a} integral_0^alpha H(A_a(theta)) 3^{-theta} dtheta + (finite terms),

where A_a(theta) = sum_{k<=a} omega^{-k} g(floor(k alpha + theta)) is the horizontal walk
started at phase theta. Equivalently Z = sum_a 2^{-a} V(A_a, a mod 3): a base-2 series whose
digits are the vertical cone readouts V(s,i) = sum_b (1 - 3F(s + B_b + corr)) 3^{-b}. For s deep
in a cone direction V(s,i) is an explicit rational up to 3^{-b*(s)}, with b*(s) the first time
the vertical walk reaches height |s|. Corrections I, P were not carried through this formula.

Further arguments tried and dropped:

7. Deep-excursion approximants from the one-walk formula: truncate both walks at times after
   which they stay deep. The approximant has an S-unit denominator 2^T 3^T' and needs both walks
   deep from bit-time T to beyond 2T. That happens only on a resonant drift with
   a_{n+1} of order 9 r_0 or more (r_0 the needed depth). This is the existing resonant model,
   with the same digit requirement.
8. Integrality of F(N): F(N) = sum_d (1-eta_d) #{m <= N/d, gcd(m,6)=1} is an integer for every
   N, and E(N) = F(N) - cN = O(log^2 N). Rational c forces E(N) in (1/B)Z, but for N = 2^A 3^B
   this is the statement N*Z in (1/B)Z + Z[1/6] again. No independent information.
9. Fourier form: M is the constant term of P(z,w) P'(z,w) / ((1-z)(1-w)) with P, P' the
   characteristic functions of the two occupation measures (multiplicative cocycle sums). The
   periodic models are the same rational functions; no cheaper denominator appears.
10. Different convergents for the two walks, or periodising one walk and truncating the other:
    cost is always at least the error exponent.

Conclusion of both rounds [H]: every complete approximant found costs at least its window
unless alpha has a partial quotient >= 5 (nonresonant) or of order 6 or more (resonant) at that
scale. A proof of (ii) along these lines is a theorem about the continued fraction of
log2/log3. Nothing here proves that such a theorem is necessary for irrationality of c.

## 8. Third round: nonvanishing replaced by a divisibility condition [P, from the working inputs]

This removes the state-class hypothesis from the conditional finish. It does not supply the
large digits.

**Lemma 8.1.** Let K be an infinite set of nonresonant indices with a_{n+1} >= 5. Suppose
Z = A/B in lowest terms. Then for all sufficiently large n in K: z_n = Z, and B divides D_n.

*Proof.* Q - 5q = (a_{n+1}-5)q + q_{n-1} >= q_{n-1} -> infinity, so
|D_n (Z - z_n)| <= 486(1+o(1)) 2^{5q-Q} -> 0. The number B D_n (Z - z_n) = A D_n - B N_n is an
integer of absolute value < 1 for large n, hence zero. Then B N_n = A D_n with gcd(A,B) = 1,
so B | D_n. No property of the first-mediant state is used. ∎

**Lemma 8.2.** Let B > 447174 = 2 * 27 * 49 * 169 divide D_n = 27(2^{3q}-1)(3^{3p}-1). Then at
least one of the following holds for (p,q) = (p_n,q_n):
- some prime l | B outside {2,3,7,13} has m_l | q or m'_l | p, where
  m_l = ord_l(2)/gcd(ord_l(2),3) >= 2 and m'_l = ord_l(3)/gcd(ord_l(3),3) >= 2;
- 4 | B and p is even;
- 81 | B and q is even;
- 343 | B and (7 | q or p even);
- 2197 | B and (13 | p or 4 | q).

*Proof.* ord_l(2) | 3 only for l = 7 and ord_l(3) | 3 only for l in {2,13}, which gives
m_l, m'_l >= 2 and the first case. Otherwise B = 2^i 3^j 7^k 13^l. Lifting the exponent gives
v_2(D_n) = 1 for p odd; v_3(D_n) = 3 for q odd; v_7(D_n) = 1 + v_7(q) + [p even](1 + v_7(p));
v_13(D_n) = 1 + v_13(p) + [4 | q](1 + v_13(q)). If none of the four listed cases holds then
i <= 1, j <= 3, k <= 2, l <= 2 and B <= 447174. ∎

**Theorem 8.3 (conditional).** Use the certified bound den(Z) >= 2^56 (it rests on the working
model theorem and the 53/84 certificate). Suppose there is an infinite set K of nonresonant
indices with a_{n+1} >= 5 such that

  (ND) for every integer M >= 2, infinitely many n in K have gcd(p_n, M) = gcd(q_n, M) = 1.

Then Z, hence c, is irrational.

*Proof.* Suppose Z = A/B in lowest terms. Then B >= 2^56 > 447174, and by 8.1 B | D_n for all
large n in K. The fixed integer B determines finitely many moduli: m_l and m'_l for the primes
l | B outside {2,3,7,13}, together with 2, 4, 7, 13. Let M be the product of all of them. By
(ND) there is a large n in K with p_n and q_n both coprime to M. For that n no m_l divides q_n,
no m'_l divides p_n, p_n and q_n are odd, 7 does not divide q_n and 13 does not divide p_n. So
none of the five cases of 8.2 holds, contradicting B | D_n. ∎

What this buys. The roadmap required, outside the six eligible classes, a proof of
infinitely-often nonvanishing on the same family as the supply. 8.3 replaces that proof by
(ND), a statement about residues of (p_n, q_n) only: no response state, no H value, no
balanced or resonant mediant analysis. Combined with the existing theorems:

  if infinitely many nonresonant n have a_{n+1} >= 5, then either Z is irrational, or
  Z = A/B and every late such n is non-eligible and has B | D_n.

What it does not buy. The supply "infinitely many nonresonant n with a_{n+1} >= 5" is still
open, and (ND) on a sparse K is not automatic.

[C] divisibility_check.py, first 590 convergents (mpmath at 1500 digits; the digits agree with
the interval-certified list in codex_review_check.py as far as they were compared, 16 terms).
There are 125 nonresonant indices with a_{n+1} >= 5. Among primes 5 <= l < 20000 only 7 and 13
divide D_n for all of them, and no further prime divides D_n on the whole second half. The most
persistent other prime below 2000 is 73, dividing 57 of 125. p_n is odd for 83, q_n odd for 77.
So no obstruction of the 8.2 type is visible on the prefix. This is finite evidence only.

## 10. Fourth round: reapproach through the x2 x3 orbit [D, C]

Fresh start, not using slope models. **No proof of (ii) resulted.** What it gives is one
framework in which every known clearer is the same object, the measured reason the framework
needs a large digit, and one more negative scan.

**10.1 Orbit form [D].** Z is rational iff the set {2^A 3^B Z mod 1} is finite (Furstenberg:
otherwise it is dense). With N = 2^A 3^B and c = 1 - 3 eta,

    N Z = integer + U + V + W,
    U = sum_{a<=A, b>B} c_{a,b} 2^{A-a} 3^{B-b}      (column wing),
    V = sum_{a>A, b<=B} c_{a,b} 2^{A-a} 3^{B-b}      (row wing),
    W = sum_{i,j>=1} c_{A+i,B+j} 2^{-i} 3^{-j}       (corner readout, |W| <= 2).

More generally, for any integer d, d Z = sum c_{a,b} d/(2^a 3^b), and each term may be reduced
mod 1 because c is an integer. So ||dZ|| is governed by the pairing of the birth array with the
signed fractional parts of d/(2^a 3^b). The size of d never enters; only how far a mismatch in
the array is amplified by those fractional parts.

**10.2 Every known clearer in this language [D].** If the wings are explicit (births constant
there) and the corner array repeats the original array, then (N-1) Z is an explicit number
mod 1 up to the first corner mismatch: a clearer N-1 with an S-unit N. The resonant model
(W-1) is this. The nonresonant model is the case where the wings are not explicit but repeat:
U ~ (2^{3q}-1) Z, V ~ (3^{3p}-1) Z, W ~ Z for N = 2^{3q} 3^{3p}, giving D_n Z ~ integer. The
horizontal mismatch at column q+Q occurs in every row b < 3p, including row 0 whose weight in V
is 3^{3p}. That amplification, 2^{3q} on each side, is the whole 6q cost. Repeating the
two-return computation with arbitrary return times of the two walks gives the same inequality
Q > 5q; it is the same count, not a new barrier theorem.

**10.3 When are wings explicit? [C]** walks.py computes the true axis walks (floors valid for
a < 301994, b < 190537) in the cone coordinates (u,v):

- horizontal walk, 300000 steps, 206 states: u, v in [-1,3] for a < 1024; [-2,3] x [-3,3] for
  a < 4096; down to -16 for a < 65536, where it stays;
- vertical walk, 190000 steps, 215 states: within [-2,2] for b < 1024; up to 18 for b < 16384.

Both walks hover at the cone apex until the resonant digit 23 at 665/1054, then drift to depth
about 14 and stay there through the digits 2, 2, 1, 1 that follow. A wing is explicit only when
one walk is deeper than the whole range of the other. On this data that happens only after a
resonant large digit, and the two walks mirror each other (their sum stays at the cone
boundary), so both wings are never explicit at once without the resonant drift. The orbit route
therefore returns the resonant model and its digit requirement.

A side effect explains the effective windows of theory.md 5.6: once the walks are deep, a
floor mismatch in one walk changes no birth in the early rows of the other, so the amplification
is 3^{3p - b_min} instead of 3^{3p}. That lengthens W_eff by the level at which the other walk
reached its present depth, which is an earlier scale, never the 4q to 5q needed at small digits.

**10.4 Structure scan of the convergents of Z [C].** cf_structure.py takes all 71933 certified
convergent denominators Q_k of Z and scores max over |r| <= 8 of v_2(Q_k - r) + 1.585 v_3(Q_k - r).
The generic maximum is 29.4 bits. The only structured convergent is index 10706, the 665/1054
resonant model (v_2 = 9282, v_3 = 3621, score 15021 bits). So up to denominators of 150991
bits, no best approximation of Z other than the known resonant one is an S-unit multiple plus a
small remainder.

Dependency remark: zcf.py initialises its recurrence as (0,1), which generates numerators, and
labels the result a denominator. The last numerator and the last denominator both have 150991
bits, so the stated bound den(Z) >= 2^150990 is unaffected.

**10.5 Other ideas tried this round and dropped.**
- Liouville with irrational algebraic approximants (nonvanishing would be free): needs error
  exponent / degree -> infinity; the natural algebraic model lives in Q(3^{1/q}), degree q.
- Adamczewski-Faverjon independence of base-2 and base-3 Mahler values: the occupation series
  are not Mahler functions because alpha is not quadratic.
- Borel-Dwork / Bezivin rationality criterion: no integer sequence with the required analytic
  continuation; rationality of Z only enters through the periodic sequence 2^A P mod R.
- Cantor-series form along a lattice path: digits are of size 3^k, no criterion applies.
- Partial fractions z_n = a/(27X) + b/(27Y): the split is by a modular inverse and does not
  converge with n.
- Displaced double returns (corner self-similarity with opposite displacements): displacement
  per resonant period is one unit, so depth k needs k periods inside the window, a digit again.

## 7. Remaining obstruction, stated exactly

Unchanged in substance: large partial quotients of alpha are still needed. Any one of the following would finish:
- infinitely many nonresonant n with a_{n+1} >= 5 satisfying (ND) of Section 8 (no state class needed);
- infinitely many nonresonant n with a_{n+1} >= 5 in an eligible state class;
- a complete clearer of cost < q_n + q_{n+1} at infinitely many indices, with nonvanishing
  there. Sections 2 to 4 show it is not a sub-product of D_n, not a small-coefficient
  polynomial of degree <= 3 in (2^q, 3^p), and not an Eisenstein factor;
- the lower bound on ||B W_n|| of Section 5.

Not checked: the corrections I, P and the low rows in Section 4 (the derivation is for the raw
colour sum); the constant C in Section 5; any audit of this note.
