# Adaptive shapes extend Erdős’s exact head

**A complete rational coupling now agrees with the true optimum through \(2^{16}\), including every boundary repair and infinite tail, but the infinite arithmetic return remains unproved.** The construction uses a genuinely enlarged palette with collinear and nonunimodular shapes, and 32 active weights in sevenths. A separate finite matching reaches \(2^{19}\); its cheap periodic extension remains missing. A new minimum-color identity replaces exponentially many head conditions by polynomially many local inequalities. All earlier continued fractions and bounded two-term combinations remain arithmetically affordable. Exact obstruction certificates delimit the tested architecture, while a counterexample at \(306/485\) breaks a tempting universal return argument. Both fresh audits pass; Erdős #168(ii) and the active goal remain open. ([Arithmetic audit](<adaptive_arithmetic_audit.md>), [witness audit](<adaptive_witness_audit.md>))

## A complete rational coupling reaches 65536

Let \(c\) be the limiting maximum density of sets avoiding every triple \(\{n,2n,3n\}\). The earlier finite theorem gives a positive token model: \((a,b)\) has color \(a-b\bmod3\), usually arrives at \(2^a3^b\), and retains the dyadic and row-one delays, including \(12\mapsto27/2\). If \(T_j(t)\) counts arrived tokens, the optimum completed count is \(\tau(t)=\min_jT_j(t)\). Its optimal reciprocal completion cost is \(M=3(1-c)\), so irrationality of \(M\) proves the target. These are accepted proved dependencies. ([Foundation](<../fractional-farey-transport/REPORT.md>))

At spans \((H,V)=(8,5)\), bounded combinations of earlier continued-fraction vectors give 28 directions. Opposite-color pairs, reflections and eligible collinear progressions produce **118 normalized shapes and 4248 anchored period-six types**. The exact witness assigns 32 types positive weights in \((1/7)\mathbb Z\), with unit coverage at every residue node. All wholly quadrant-contained triples are retained; clipped mass lies in the boundary strips. ([Palette and explicit weights](<adaptive_capacity.md>))

The repair uses cuts \(A=18,B=12\), beyond the minimal period-rounded cuts. Residual masses per color are \(239/7\) in the corner, \(50/7\) in the bottom root block and \(52/7\) in the left root block. Actual-arrival rank matching balances each component; bottom groups repeat with time factor 64 and left groups with factor 729. Complete bulk streams also retain their exceptional first members and both geometric continuations. The resulting **full infinite cost** is
\[
Q=\frac{20605867009283}{34511190884352},
\qquad
D=7(63\cdot728\cdot2^6 3^6)2^{18}3^{12}
=2086759741982823677952,\quad DQ\in\mathbb Z.
\]
All buffers are charged in this 71-bit safe clearing factor. It is an upper clearing factor, not a reduced-denominator lower bound. ([Complete sum and clearance](<adaptive_capacity.md>))

Direct replay gives \(G(t)=\tau(t)\) at **all 80 actual events through \(2^{16}\)**, including simultaneous arrivals and \(27/2\). The first deficit is one at 73728. This extends the earlier small fractional example’s head from 64 to 65536 and supplies a genuine strict rational comparison \(Q<M\). Its fully charged denominator still prevents treating this finite success as an adequate arithmetic return. ([Independent cost and event reconstruction](<adaptive_witness_audit.md>))

## Local trades extend a different finite head

Equal-rank triples are not the only optimal matching. At ranks 27–29, cycling three color-zero partners produces
\[
\{(13,1),(10,3),(14,0)\},\quad
\{(4,7),(12,2),(7,5)\},\quad
\{(2,8),(1,9),(9,4)\}.
\]
These use the same nine tokens, fit the \(8\times5\) box and preserve completion times \(27648,36864,41472\) individually. The exchange therefore preserves every completed count and its reciprocal cost. An oversized equal-rank triple alone cannot establish a universal span obstruction. ([Exact alternating trade](<adaptive_structure.md>))

Further exact matching yields **43 disjoint triples using ten normalized shapes**, covering the first 43 tokens of every color and matching every actual optimum count through \(2^{19}\). The next balanced reset, from 43 to 44, forces \(\{(0,12),(8,7),(16,2)\}\), which exceeds that box. This is a stronger finite head than the complete rational example above, but supplies neither a periodic whole-plane extension nor a cheap infinite rational cost. ([Finite certificate and independent replay](<adaptive_witness_audit.md>))

The geometry matters. At the earlier obstruction token \((12,5)\), the only bounded-span admissible triple is \(\{(12,5),(15,3),(18,1)\}\), a collinear progression along \((3,-2)\). The first traded shape also uses the non-CF direction \((4,-3)=(1,-1)+(3,-2)\). Its alignment exploits low-row retimings; vertical repetition removes them and leaves raw dispersion \(\log(27/16)\). Thus a useful boundary exchange cannot automatically become a valid repeated interior shape. ([Forced collinear shape and timing limitation](<adaptive_structure.md>))

## Minimum-color conservation makes larger palettes affordable

The arithmetic theorem now allows any finite palette of distinct three-color shapes inside a common \(H\times V\) box, including collinear shapes and determinant greater than one. With period \(m\) divisible by six, unit residue coverage gives unit plane marginals. Explicit color balance in the corner and both root edge blocks permits complete separate rank repairs. If \(S\) clears all weights, cumulative masses and their differences remain in \(S^{-1}\mathbb Z\): splitting pays **one factor \(S\)**. The full factor is
\[
D=S(2^m-1)(3^m-1)2^m3^m\,2^A3^B.
\]
Larger cuts use their actual \(A,B\); unbounded buffers cannot be hidden in an \(O(m)\) overhead. ([General construction and fresh audit](<adaptive_arithmetic_audit.md>))

A conservation identity makes actual head feasibility simpler. Let \(G_B\) be completed bulk mass, \(P_j\) arrived color-\(j\) mass reserved in incomplete bulk triples, and \(C_{E,j}\) arrived residual mass in repair component \(E\). Choose any actual minimum color \(j\), with \(T_j=\tau\). Then
\[
\boxed{\tau-G=P_j+\sum_E\left(C_{E,j}-\min_\ell C_{E,\ell}\right).}
\]
Every term is nonnegative. Consequently head equality is equivalent to \(P_j=0\) and \(C_{E,j}\le C_{E,\ell}\) for every component and color. The first condition bans types with positive incomplete minimum-color profile; the second gives local linear inequalities. This checks the **actual specified repair**, including later edge blocks, and replaces the earlier exponential combinations of component minima. ([Proof and polynomial constraint count](<adaptive_arithmetic.md>))

For \(X\le2^{KH}\), the formulation has polynomially many head rows with polynomial integer coefficients. Coverage bounds the weight polytope. If nonempty, it has a rational vertex cleared by one active determinant. With \(s\) positive vertex coordinates and coefficient bound \(L\), one can use \(S\le(\sqrt{s}\,CL)^s\). Sparsity is useful when present, but no general sparsity theorem is assumed. ([Support-sensitive determinant audit](<adaptive_arithmetic_audit.md>))

All earlier convergents are affordable: \(q_{i+2}\ge2q_i\) gives only \(O(\log H)\) vectors, yielding \(O(\log^2H)\) pair/progression shapes and \(\log S=O(\log^3H)\) at fixed period. Bounded two-term combinations give \(O(\log^4H)\) shapes and \(\log S=O(\log^5H)\). Both overheads are \(o(H)\). Older directions need not individually approach equal arrival times; the enclosing box controls arithmetic, and candidatewise dispersion supplies strictness. **These bounds establish affordability, not infinite feasibility.** ([Palette generation and conditional theorem](<adaptive_arithmetic.md>))

## Exact certificates limit the tested architecture

The 118-shape buffered class fails through \(2^{17}\). Actual zero-surplus bans leave 165 types; every removed original type has a replayable minimum-color witness. On the survivors, a hardcoded 42-row integer dual for coverage and component balance satisfies
\[
y^Tb=83>0,\qquad y^TE\le0.
\]
Nonnegative weights would imply \(83=y^TEx\le0\). This handles all 4248 original types without trusting an optimizer. It excludes the stated palette, period and decomposition, without excluding larger palettes, other periods, cut phases or mixed-component repairs. ([Full dual replay](<adaptive_witness_audit.md>))

The minimal-cut obstruction has a different logical scope. Support restrictions from the **entire \(2^{16}\) head** leave 339 types. With \(A=12,B=6\), they force left-block arrived masses \(C_0=1,C_1=0\) at 729, although zero is a global minimum color. This contradicts that requested full head; it does not locate the first unrestricted infeasibility threshold at 729. The buffered positive witness shows why changing components helps. Every claimed flux obstruction likewise includes the relevant head restrictions: coverage and balance alone can be feasible. ([Conditioned component argument](<adaptive_capacity.md>))

Balanced resets give a palette-independent support test. If all three counts rise from \(k\) to \(k+1\), exactly three new tokens arrive. Optimality at the earlier endpoint consumes all old mass, forcing the three new tokens together at the later endpoint, even for fractional couplings. For the period-six architecture, oversized forced triples cannot fit the bulk or either narrow edge block and must fit the corner. At bulk boxes \((27,17)\) and \((84,53)\), the specified two-span heads require respectively \(A\ge53,B\ge34\) and \(A\ge168,B\ge96\); six-rounded cuts are \((54,36)\) and \((168,96)\). These are **support necessities**, not reduced-denominator lower bounds or universal impossibility theorems. ([Forced-reset proof and corner certificates](<adaptive_structure.md>))

A tempting generalization fails. The progression
\[
\{(0,2p+2),(q,p+2),(2q,2)\}
\]
gives balanced resets at \(5/8,12/19,41/65,53/84\). At the actual convergent \(306/485\), its three arrivals remain consecutive, but counts change from \((99852,99851,99852)\) to \((99853,99852,99853)\). Neither endpoint is balanced, despite the next denominator \(1054>2\cdot485\). Existing surplus prevents the forced-reset argument. Four small examples therefore cannot establish an automatic continued-fraction return theorem. ([Exact counterexample and independent row counts](<adaptive_witness_audit.md>))

## Infinite feasibility remains the decisive theorem

The sufficient target remains one actual infinite family with head \(X=2^{(2+\varepsilon)H}\), for fixed \(\varepsilon>0\), and fully charged \(\log D=2H\log2+o(H)\). Actual head equality bounds \(M-Q=O(\log X/X)\); strictness then gives
\[
DQ\in\mathbb Z,\qquad
0<D(M-Q)\le \exp(o(H))\,O(H)2^{-\varepsilon H}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Rational \(M=a/b\) would make \(bD(M-Q)\) positive integers tending to zero. The enlarged palettes now have sufficient denominator control for this implication. **Their infinite actual feasibility remains unproved.** The older signed hierarchy separately retains its boundary-arithmetic and nonvanishing gaps. ([Conditional finish and limits](<adaptive_arithmetic_audit.md>))

Three supplied exact checkers and two independent checkers pass alongside the written proofs. Independent verification reconstructs the complete cost using integer-time mass clones, replays every original obstruction column and finite-head event, and checks mixed-shape arithmetic at periods six and twelve. These audits establish the scoped advances above. The next theorem must join a repeatable actual matching mechanism to the affordable growing palette; finite exchanges and consecutive arrivals alone do not supply it. ([Arithmetic audit](<adaptive_arithmetic_audit.md>), [witness audit](<adaptive_witness_audit.md>))
