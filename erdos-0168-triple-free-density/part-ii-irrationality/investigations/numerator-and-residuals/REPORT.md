# Positive occupations isolate Erdős’s remaining arithmetic

**A genuinely infinite family of positive real returns is now proved for the actual logarithm ratio.** Complete occupation products express its members as four translated cone values; actual-axis unboundedness supplies infinitude, and positivity supplies nonvanishing. Their decay has an explicit exponential bound. An integer-affine identity with the density target would convert this family into an irrationality proof, but **that arithmetic bridge has not been constructed**. Two other results sharpen the fan route: complete numerator congruences locate the required savings on both period axes, and a new theorem proves corrected-fan nonvanishing on a specified following-digit subfamily. Three independent audits pass with no substantive erratum, supported by three supplied and three standalone independent exact checkers. **No adequate infinite arithmetic return or proof of Erdős #168(ii) is established.** ([Occupation audit](<occupation_audit.md>), [numerator audit](<numerator_audit.md>), [residual audit](<residual_audit.md>))

Let \(c=\lim F(N)/N\) be the limiting optimal density of sets avoiding every complete triple \(\{n,2n,3n\}\). The accepted finite theorem gives
\[
\tau(D_t)=\min_j(K_j-I_j-P_j),\qquad
D_t=\{2^a3^b\le t:a,b\ge0\},\quad t\ge3,
\]
with the isolate and patch corrections retained. Its birth digits \(\eta_{a,b}\in\{0,1\}\) give
\[
M=\sum_{a,b\ge0}\eta_{a,b}2^{-a}3^{-b},\qquad
Z=3-3M=9c-6.
\]
Thus irrationality of \(Z\) proves the density assertion. The finite optimum, density reduction and complete scalar models are written theorem inputs with earlier internal audits. ([Finite foundation](<../prefix-and-phase-audit/REPORT.md>), [scalar arithmetic](<../signed-arithmetic-returns/REPORT.md>))

**Nine occupation products retain the whole target and response family.** Work on \(\mathbb Z[\omega]\), where \(\omega^2+\omega+1=0\), and write \(\delta_z\) for unit mass at state \(z\). The actual unexceptional state separates as
\[
\zeta_{a,b}=A_a+B_b-1+C_{a,b}-\mathbf1_{b\ge4}E_b.
\]
Here \(A_a,B_b\) are the actual axes, \(C_{a,b}\) is the residue-dependent color correction, and \(E_b\) is the high-row isolate/patch correction. For \(i,j\in\{0,1,2\}\), define
\[
\mathsf A_i=\sum_{a\equiv i(3)}2^{-a}\delta_{A_a},\qquad
\mathsf B_j=\sum_{\substack{b\ge4\\b\equiv j(3)}}3^{-b}\delta_{B_b-1-E_b}.
\]
The target high-row occupation is exactly
\[
\nu^H=\sum_{i,j=0}^2\delta_{C_{ij}}*\mathsf A_i*\mathsf B_j,
\qquad \|\nu^H\|_1=\frac1{27}.
\]
Canonical endpoints rotate the horizontal measure by a cube root and change the finite residue shifts. This same construction represents all eight canonical fan responses. Low rows replace the vertical measure by a single weighted atom, while retaining their entire infinite horizontal sums. Absolute convergence justifies every convolution and regrouping. This is a product-measure identity; it does not factor a cone value into two known rational scalar values. ([Complete products RA1–RA3](<response_arithmetic.md>), [independent reconstruction](<occupation_audit.md>))

Put \(F(x+y\omega)=\mathbf1_{\{x\le0,\ x-y\le0\}}\) and
\[
Q_H(t)=\langle F(\cdot+t),\nu^H\rangle,\qquad
Q_{\rm full}(t)=Q_H(t)+L(t),
\]
where \(L\) retains the four complete low-row readouts. The word “full” here means all rows of the **unexceptional** rule. The original birth override at \((0,1)\) has weight \(1/3\), so the exact target normalization is
\[
\boxed{M=Q_{\rm full}(0)+\frac13,\qquad
Z=2-3Q_{\rm full}(0),\qquad Q_H=Q_{\rm full}-L.}
\tag{1}
\]
The constant is two. The independent checker verifies it against original finite-board births and reproduces complete rational model values, including the exception exactly once. ([Normalization audit](<occupation_audit.md>))

For \(D_vf(t)=f(t)-f(t-v)\), the high-row Green identity is
\[
D_\omega D_{\omega^2}F=\delta_0.
\]
**This basic stencil is archival:** an equivalent translated form already appears in H12–H13. The present complete row formulation also proves
\[
D_1D_\omega\Phi_1=\delta_1,\qquad
D_\omega D_{\omega^2}\Phi_2=\delta_{\omega^2},\qquad
D_1D_{\omega^2}\Phi_3=-\delta_{-\omega},\qquad \Phi_0=0.
\tag{2}
\]
The negative row-three sign matters. These are derivatives of a common minimum potential \(m(x,y)=\min(0,x,y)\), with \(F=D_1m\); bounded axis increments and finite weighted first moments ensure convergence. Applied to the high measure, the stencil gives
\[
\boxed{D_\omega D_{\omega^2}Q_H(t)=\nu^H(-t).}
\tag{3}
\]
Applying it to \(Q_{\rm full}\) instead requires subtracting the same differences of \(L\). State translation is not physical index translation, and those low rows are not automatically rational. ([Archival stencil](<../hybrid-arithmetic-closure/hybrid_compression.md>), [complete kernels and audit](<occupation_audit.md>))

**Actual unboundedness produces infinitely many positive small atoms.** Define the original color imbalance \(\mathcal K(t)=\sum_{j=0}^2\omega^jK_j(t)\). Original-board summation gives \(A_a=\omega^{-a}\mathcal K(2^a)\), hence \(A_{3m}=\mathcal K(2^{3m})\). The audit independently reconstructed its unboundedness on this precise actual orbit. With \(\beta=\log_2 3\) and \(\ell_m=\lfloor3m/\beta\rfloor\), the exact row sum is
\[
(1-\omega)\mathcal K(2^{3m})
=\sum_{b=0}^{\ell_m}\omega^{-b}
-\omega\sum_{b=0}^{\ell_m}\omega^{-\lceil(\beta+1)b\rceil}.
\tag{4}
\]
The first sum is bounded. The real part of the second is a fixed-start discrepancy for a third of the circle under rotation by \(\theta=(\beta+1)/3\). Kelly–Sadun’s primary Kesten criterion makes that discrepancy unbounded because \(1/3\notin\mathbb Z\theta+\mathbb Z\). Since \(1<3/\beta<2\), successive sampled indices \(\ell_m\) differ by one or two; boundedness on the samples would bound every intervening prefix. Thus **the actual \(A_{3m}\) is unbounded**, with no generic-phase substitution. ([Kelly–Sadun, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/1404.0455), [independent actual-orbit derivation](<occupation_audit.md>))

At fixed \(b=4,a=3m\), every residue and patch offset is constant, so \(\zeta_{3m,4}=A_{3m}+\text{constant}\). The positive measure \(\nu^H\) therefore has infinitely many occupied states. For an occupied state define its first total-index arrival
\[
N(z)=\min\{a+b:b\ge4,\ \zeta_{a,b}=z\}.
\]
Only finitely many cells lie below any fixed total-index bound. We can consequently select distinct \(z_n\) with \(N_n=N(z_n)\to\infty\). Every contribution to that atom lies beyond its first-arrival triangle, giving the complete tail
\[
\boxed{0<\nu^H(z_n)\le
\sum_{\substack{a,b\ge0\\a+b\ge N_n}}2^{-a}3^{-b}
=6\,2^{-N_n}-3\,3^{-N_n}\longrightarrow0.}
\tag{5}
\]
The full-quadrant sum safely overbounds the high rows; splitting at \(b=N_n\) evaluates it exactly. No quantitative escape-time estimate is asserted. Infinitude comes from (4), not the finite arrival checks. ([Support, selection and tail proof RA8–RA10](<response_arithmetic.md>), [independent audit](<occupation_audit.md>))

Combining (3)–(5) yields the actual positive real returns
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathcal E_n={}&Q_H(-z_n)-Q_H(-z_n-\omega)
-Q_H(-z_n-\omega^2)+Q_H(1-z_n)\\
={}&\nu^H(z_n)>0,\qquad
\mathcal E_n\le6\,2^{-N_n}-3\,3^{-N_n}.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{6}
\]
Their infinitude, positivity and decay are proved together. The missing arithmetic is specific: suppose one could prove
\[
T_n\nu^H(z_n)=u_nZ+v_n,\qquad
T_n\in\mathbb Z_{>0},\quad u_n,v_n\in\mathbb Z,\quad
\log T_n=o(N_n).
\tag{7}
\]
For hypothetical \(Z=A_*/B_*\), the number \(B_*T_n\nu^H(z_n)\) would be a positive integer tending to zero. More generally \(\log_2T_n\le(1-\varepsilon)N_n\) suffices. No separate height restriction on \(u_n,v_n\) is needed for this fixed-denominator argument. **Equation (7) remains an unproved bridge, not a consequence of the Green identity.** ([Conditional deduction RA11](<occupation_audit.md>))

There is no free lattice for these atoms. Infinite positive support and total mass \(1/27\) exclude one integer clearing them all. If an atom is rational, its reduced denominator exceeds \(2^{N(z)}/6\) by (5). This proves no specified atom irrational and does not exclude the target-dependent identity (7) when \(Z\) is irrational. It explains why rational total masses, one known target cone value, and positivity do not supply the translated arithmetic. ([Arithmetic limitations RA12](<occupation_audit.md>))

**The complete model numerator localizes savings to four genuine period residues.** For consecutive actual convergents \(p/q,P/Q\) of \(\alpha=\log2/\log3\), put \(\sigma=\operatorname{sign}(\alpha-p/q)\) and set
\[
a=2^q,\quad b=3^p,\quad U=2^Q,\quad V=3^P,\quad
A=a^3-1,\quad B=b^3-1.
\]
The nonresonant model \(z=N/(27AB)\) has a \(3q\) horizontal period, a \(3p\) high-row period, four separate low rows, and the origin restored once. Let \(\bar c\) be the periodic coefficient rule before that override. For \(i,j\in\{0,1,2\}\), define
\[
H_i=\sum_{0\le r<q}\sum_{v=0}^3
\bar c_{r+iq,v}2^{q-1-r}3^{3-v},\qquad
T_{ij}=\sum_{0\le r<q}\sum_{0\le t<p}
c_{r+iq,t+jp+4}2^{q-1-r}3^{p-1-t}.
\]
Writing \(W_i=BH_i+\sum_jb^{2-j}T_{ij}\) and \(Y_j=\sum_i a^{2-i}T_{ij}\), complete geometric summation gives
\[
\boxed{N=2\sum_i a^{2-i}W_i-27AB
=2B\sum_i a^{2-i}H_i+2\sum_jb^{2-j}Y_j-27AB.}
\tag{8}
\]
The last term is the restored scalar correction \(-1\). The head survives horizontally and vanishes from vertical residues. ([Nine-block proof NN1–NN2](<numerator.md>), [independent full-period audit](<numerator_audit.md>))
